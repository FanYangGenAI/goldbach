# Multiplicative countermodel (2026-11-28)
Read hub summary eccb086 and prior uniform countermodel cf26eba. Let p=3 mod 4 be prime, chi the Legendre symbol, b=1 on F_p^*, 0 at zero, f=b+chi. Then chi is completely multiplicative (including zero), chi(-1)=-1, f>=0, and f(x)f(-x)=0 identically. Thus (f*f)(0)=0 but (b*b)(0)=p-1. The support of f is itself a multiplicative subgroup. For k nonzero the unnormalized additive Fourier transform of chi has modulus sqrt(p): squaring the Gauss sum and substituting x=ty for y nonzero gives sum_t chi(t) sum_{y!=0} e(k(t-1)y/p)=p. At k=0 it vanishes. Fourier completion of any interval of length <=p gives discrepancy O(sqrt(p) log p), since the interval transform is bounded by min(H,p/(2 min(k,p-k))) for nonzero k. The same holds for arithmetic progressions with step coprime to p by permutation of frequencies. Hence even complete multiplicativity of the residual, square-root additive Fourier uniformity, and approximate progression uniformity do not suffice for binary positivity. Unlike previous model, exact local marginals are not asserted. To impose odd support use CRT on Z/(2p), restrict to odd residues and lift chi; ordinary sum 2p then has no representation. This is not a prime model: it has an explicit high-conductor character obstruction and no claim of nonpretentiousness or prime density.
