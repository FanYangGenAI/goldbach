# Scale-dependent multiplicative perturbation (2026-11-28)
Read hub summary eccb086 and multiplicative countermodel 7d3a1fe. New elementary obstruction: let N be even and define completely multiplicative g_N by g_N(p)=+1 for N/2<p<=N, and -1 otherwise. On [1,N], g_N differs from Liouville only at those primes (no multiples fit). Thus for any fixed positive affine forms L_i(n) whose values lie in [1,N], the difference of their k-fold correlation sums is bounded by 2k*pi(N)=O_k(N/log N), by injectivity of each form and telescoping. Same bound for reflection n,N-n. For every Dirichlet character chi and real t, the squared pretentious distances up to N differ by at most 2 sum_{N/2<p<=N}1/p=O(1/log N). Yet for every prime pair p+q=N with p!=q, g_N(p)g_N(q)=-1 and at least one factor has sign +1, unlike lambda. If N/2 composite, no prime pair has both signs -1. This does not change the actual prime set, and does not disprove any fixed-function Elliott theorem: g_N varies with N. It shows that unweighted correlation estimates with error larger than N/log N and pretentious-distance tests alone cannot distinguish this scale-dependent parity perturbation on the prime-pair support. For a fixed N the function is a finite prime modification of lambda. The usual nonpretentiousness of lambda is not proved here or needed for the distance comparison.
