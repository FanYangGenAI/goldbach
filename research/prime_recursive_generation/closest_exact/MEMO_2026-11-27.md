## Method and limitations

For each m=N/2 find the smallest d>=0 with m-d and m+d prime; p=m-d, q=m+d. Sieve residue classes 1 and 5 mod 6, handle small primes separately, use OpenMP and 64-bit masks. Bit-parallel search cap d=100000; zero unresolved means every center is covered and the first hit is globally closest. If unresolved is nonzero do not interpret the fit as full-range. Define A=sum((m-2)d), B=sum(d^2), L=X/2-2, T=L(L+1)(2L+1)/6. Exact fit a=1+2(A-B)/(T-2A+B). Use 128-bit integer accumulators. No GPU or restart checkpoints. The executable is not committed.
